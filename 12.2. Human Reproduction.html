04 · Hormonal control

Who stimulates whom?

Male axis shortcut

LH → Leydig cells → androgens. FSH → Sertoli cells → factors supporting spermiogenesis.

Female axis shortcut

FSH supports follicular growth; rising follicular estrogen helps trigger the mid-cycle LH surge; LH induces ovulation and corpus-luteum formation.

06 · Fertilisation

Site, recognition and meiotic completion

Fertilisation normally occurs at the ampullary–isthmic junction of the oviduct.
Sperm contact with the zona pellucida triggers changes that help prevent entry of additional sperm.
Acrosomal enzymes help the sperm pass through the egg coverings; the sperm membrane then fuses with the oocyte membrane.
The secondary oocyte completes meiosis II, producing the ovum and second polar body.
Male and female pronuclei fuse, restoring the diploid chromosome number in the zygote.
Sex determination

The ovum always carries X. A fertilising sperm carries X or Y; therefore the sperm’s chromosome determines XX or XY.

Location condition

Fertilisation is possible only when sperm and secondary oocyte reach the ampullary–isthmic junction at the same time.

08 · Pregnancy and development

Tap a milestone

Placenta: exchange plus endocrine support

Exchange

Facilitates delivery of oxygen and nutrients and removal of carbon dioxide and wastes.

Attachment

Fetal chorionic villi interdigitate with maternal uterine tissue; an umbilical cord connects fetus and placenta.

Hormones

Placenta produces hCG, hPL, estrogens and progestogens. Relaxin is also detectable later in pregnancy and is secreted by the ovary.

Three germ layers

After implantation, the inner cell mass differentiates into ectoderm, mesoderm and endoderm; adult tissues and organs arise from these layers.

Parturition

Neuroendocrine mechanisms trigger childbirth at the end of gestation. Oxytocin reinforces uterine contractions in a positive-feedback loop.

Lactation

Prolactin supports milk production; oxytocin supports milk ejection. Early colostrum is rich in antibodies and helps protect the newborn.

12 · Rapid glossary

Last-minute definitions

Spermiogenesis

Transformation of haploid spermatids into spermatozoa.

Spermiation

Release of mature spermatozoa from Sertoli cells into the seminiferous-tubule lumen.

Ovulation

Release of the secondary oocyte from a mature Graafian follicle.

Menarche

The first menstrual cycle at puberty.

Menopause

Permanent cessation of menstrual cycles, generally around the late forties to early fifties.

Cleavage

Rapid mitotic divisions of the zygote that form blastomeres without major growth.

Implantation

Embedding of the blastocyst in the uterine endometrium.

Parturition

The process of childbirth at the end of gestation.
